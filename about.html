Who we are

Welcome to GreenPack!

We supply takeaway packaging to businesses of every size, from home based food vendors to established restaurant chains. GreenPack stocks a full range of compostable and biodegradable packaging, sourced and sold directly, so you get quality without the middleman markup. Whatever the size of your order, we've got you covered.

Our Story

From a simple vision in 2025 to a growing green movement, GreenPack was founded by Tendayi Machaya to transform local food service in Harare and stop the unchecked spread of microplastic contamination, which was impossible to ignore.

At GreenPack, we make high-quality, compostable packaging with zero compromises on the price or the planet.

Meet the founder

Tendayi Machaya

Founder & Director, GreenPack

Tendai Machaya is the founder of GreenPack, leading the mission to eliminate single-use plastics in Zimbabwe through affordable, biodegradable food packaging. A technology innovator with First Class Honours in Computer Science from Ashesi University, he bridges science and sustainable business to solve environmental challenges across Southern Africa.

At GreenPack, Tendai pioneered Harare’s plantable 1 Cup – 10 Seeds initiative to combine waste reduction with urban greening. His work has earned him the Youngest Environmentalist of the Year Award and the Excellence in Community Empowerment & Social Impact Award.

Previously, Tendai co-developed an AI-powered breath analyzer for early COPD detection (published in Digital Health, 2026) and competed on Ashesi’s global iGEM team, winning a gold medal and ranking in the top 10 worldwide in Entrepreneurship and Biomanufacturing.
